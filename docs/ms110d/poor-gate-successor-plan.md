# MS110D Poor-gate successor program - program plan

Registered 2026-08-20, before any DSP work, in the form [wn8-program-plan.md](wn8-program-plan.md) set (itself the Phase B form: "exactly as phase-b-plan.md registered before any of it ran"). This is the successor to both [Phase B](phase-b-closeout.md) and the [WN8 redesign program](wn8-program-plan.md); it is deliberately not "Phase C", which design §6 reserves for WN9-12. Leg IDs are `G0...G3` (gate legs, this repo's work) and `H1...H2` (hardware legs, Tom's hands); evidence dirs `evidence/YYYY-MM-DD-poorgate-g<N>/`; branches `poorgate-g<N>`.

## 1. Charter

Close, improve, or measure-infeasible the two MS110D Poor points that remain measured-only against the D-LXIV 1E-5 coded-BER mask, and discharge the record-keeping and production-path debt the two prior programs left behind:

- **WN7 (8PSK r3/4, U=256/K=32) at +19 dB**: closed by Phase B at **2.56E-5 canonical / 1.48E-5 disjoint** under the B3.9 verdict ([phase-b-closeout.md](phase-b-closeout.md) §4: "the residual is the waveform's own floor at this channel/SNR; reducing it requires added information - diversity, retransmission, outer coding - outside this demodulator").
- **WN8 (16QAM r3/4, U=256/K=32) at +23 dB**: closed by the WN8 program as exit (ii) at **2.90E-4 canonical / 1.75E-2 disjoint** through the shipped MFB-form receiver ([evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w6/](evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w6/)), from coin-flip at its entry.

**Scope is receiver-only**, as before: the wire waveform is fixed by MIL-STD-188-110D App D and stays byte-exact to spec; pilots-in-data, altered probe cadence or any TX-side change is out of scope. **Non-goals**: WN9-12 (Phase C, "ships only if a real use appears", design §6); 2G ALE (its own plan, [../ale/plan.md](../ale/plan.md)); on-air Poor gating (§6 below); protocol-layer answers to WN7 (diversity combining, ARQ integration, outer coding are real answers, but they belong to the link layer, not this program).

**Three acceptable exits per point**, verbatim from the WN8 program: (i) hard-gated at mask; (ii) improved but measured-only, the new ceiling re-banked; (iii) measured infeasible under any implementable receiver-only class. The deliverable is a measured, written verdict, not necessarily a gate flip.

## 2. Starting position - what is already measured

**WN7.** The B3.9 verdict was reached with the probe-trained T/2 DFE + tail-tracking + chain-BCJR model class, and it was internally consistent: every block of all 16 bursts converges (B3.8), every residual error is a single contiguous 14-24-info-bit decoder event with honest LLRs (wrong wire bits at mean |LLR| ~1 against 12-68 for right bits), and the oracle-labels ceiling measured worse than ship (142 vs 131), so no label-side lever existed. What has changed since is the class, not the waveform. The WN8 program's W1 leg measured that the same class's "immovable" WN8 ceiling was the estimator's segment-anchored time model, not the chains (truth-in-class 100/36 vs oracle 496/136), and W1's own calibration lane put WN7's oracle-residual guard-pin corpse under per-symbol truth: **0 coded errors on all 11 blocks, including b5, where the oracle leaves 15** ([evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w1/README.md](evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w1/README.md)). Closeout §5 says a banked negative reopens "only with new mechanism evidence"; that is new mechanism evidence. WN7's gap is 2.6x / 1.5x, 131 pooled errors per 6.49M bits, not WN8's 290x; the mask needs <= 32 errors direct on a 3.24M-bit family, or the 97.5 % Poisson bound to clear at fewer. Nobody has pointed the MFB-form receiver at WN7. Both the test-side prototype (`Ms110dMfbFormReceiver`) and the shipped decoder (`Ms110dMfbBlockDecoder`) are hard-wired to the Table D-VII QAM16 map and nibble-XOR scrambling; 8PSK needs a modulation-generic symbol mapper (tribit map per Table D-VI, scrambler added modulo 8 per D.5.1.3). About a day of instrument build, test-side, before the measurement. The physics is genuinely open: 8PSK carries no envelope information (every point on the unit circle, 45 degree spacing against 16QAM's 30 degree outer ring), so matched projection buys less than it did at 16QAM, and the old class already converged every WN7 block; what it could buy is exactly what the W2 anatomy located - the FF-sandwich floor on the deep-fade blocks' pricing and trajectory.

**WN8.** The W6 facts. The residual is one mechanism, not a diffuse floor: the disjoint 1.75E-2 is entirely three bursts whose **first block** defeats both decode schedules and reverts to coin-flip (w1/b0 24,819; w2/b0 24,465; w3/b0 24,468 errors); the fourth such block at W5b2, w0/b1, was recovered by W6a's hard-first fallback (24,242 -> 1,890). Without the three, the family sits at ~1.5E-5. Canonical is dominated by one burst leaking 1,029 (w1, channelSeed 1001509); without it ~5E-5. The slow-fade edge is measured at the 500k smoke budget: {1 ms, 0.5 Hz} 9.09E-2, {3 ms, 2 Hz} 7.55E-4 ([evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w5b2/](evidence/2026-07-31-wn8-w5b2/)). Banked unused: W3's label-free moment observables (the outer ring is an exact 12PSK, so windowed y^12 is a 30-degree-ambiguous phase track and windowed |y|^2 an envelope track, on aperiodic data); fade-adaptive refit cadence; schedule diversity beyond two. Block 0 is the block with the thinnest anchor context by construction: the per-burst delay-profile window is accumulated over its own probes, and its trajectory's left boundary has nothing before it except the autobaud preamble - a long run of known symbols the current estimator does not use as an anchor. The matched-filter bound on the exact channel is 0/0 on both specimens (W1b), so exit (iii) is permanently refuted for WN8.

**Production path.** The GB7RDG tenant plan ([../plan.md](../plan.md), 2026-08-06 entry) places `ms110d-wn6` at 3750 Hz audio through `FrequencyShiftedModem`. That wrapper is gated by a coarse AWGN frame-count ladder on WN2/WN6 only (`FrequencyShiftedModemTests`, 0.2 dB tolerance); WN7/WN8 never traverse it in any test; the Poor battery does not cover it; and nothing has gone on air at a moved centre. `ms110d-wn7` and `ms110d-wn8` are first-class catalogue modes, selectable by config and by KISS SETHW, with nothing in the product stating that their Poor performance is measured-only or that 16QAM did not carry over a 48 W NVIS path on 2026-08-03.

**Physics on air.** +19 and +23 dB sit above both rigs' ceilings: the RSP1 pad-chain rig delivered ~13.5 dB at 3.7 W ([evidence/2026-07-27-110d-full-campaign/](evidence/2026-07-27-110d-full-campaign/)), so ~19-20 dB at the 15 W now permitted; 40 m NVIS at 48 W did not carry WN8 even at its AWGN-class SNR ([evidence/2026-08-03-e4-first-on-air/](evidence/2026-08-03-e4-first-on-air/)). Any Poor gate this program produces is sim-only unless H2 lifts the pad-chain ceiling.

## 3. Discipline (binding, carried from Phase B and the WN8 program)

[wn8-program-plan.md](wn8-program-plan.md) §3 applies verbatim: registration before running (question, decision inputs, mechanism claim, instrument and method with exact seeds and env knobs, budget, pre-committed numeric kill/proceed rule); cheapest decisive measurement first; ceiling instruments before implementations; corpse before battery, battery only at leg exit or pre-merge; canonical/disjoint specimens (`channelSeed = SEED + worker x 10^6 + burst x 10^3 + 1`, SEED 500+wn canonical, +10000 disjoint); the escalation rule (a written stop-and-reassess when a stage budget closes above target, not more tuning); banked negatives as amended (closeout §5 with the W6 amendments to B3.10 and B3.4; the oracle-labels pass is a reference, not a bound; per-bin FD MMSE is dominated by the chain BCJR at equal channel knowledge); the guard pins (closeout §6, hermetic count re-pinned at G0); new instruments get a calibration lane before they are trusted; every tuned constant faces the off-rig direction checks; shipped-path changes are structurally scoped to the modulation they were measured on (8PSK for anything G1 ships, QAM16 for G2); three-lane detached batteries, fleets expendable, never rebuild mid-battery.

Two additions:

- **The byte-identity baseline is the G0 battery**, on the Release test exe, and every later leg compares against it.
- **The measured-only bank.** G0 pins WN7's and WN8's closing counts in `Ms110dMaskTests.MeasuredOnlyBank` as exact assertions in the battery configuration. A chase leg that intends to move them runs `MS110D_POOR_GATED=1` (which asserts the 1E-5 mask instead); a leg that ships re-banks with its own closing battery in the same PR. No number moves silently in either direction.

## 4. Leg ladder

- **G0 - close the books and re-baseline (S).** Registration in [evidence/2026-08-20-poorgate-g0/](evidence/2026-08-20-poorgate-g0/). The bank (above) in the test suite; the five guard-pin corpses; the three-lane battery on current main with the bank armed, so the re-baseline and the bank validate each other. Alongside, in a docs-only PR, the reconciliation the programs left undone: [phase-b-closeout.md](phase-b-closeout.md) §4/§5/§6/§9 amended with the W6 measurements (both WN8 walls fell; the hermetic pin is 790/0); [README.md](README.md) status carries the WN8 program and this one; the [mode-validation.md](../mode-validation.md) rows drop their retired caveats (#102 closed 2026-07-28, the 2026-07-27 evidence is committed); [../roadmap.md](../roadmap.md) #7 stops calling the WN8 program live; the OTA documents ([ota-handover.md](ota-handover.md), [ota-air-backlog.md](ota-air-backlog.md), [ota-execution-plan.md](ota-execution-plan.md), [ota-capture-client-plan.md](ota-capture-client-plan.md), [ota-test-plan.md](ota-test-plan.md)) state what the 2026-07-27 and 2026-08-03 evidence actually closed. Kill/proceed: any drift in the battery -> stop and diagnose before any DSP work.

- **G1 - WN7 under the MFB-form receiver (M) - the decisive cheap measurement.** Question: does the label-free MFB-form receiver, generalised to 8PSK, clear WN7's residual at the mask class on the WN7 specimens? Instrument: a modulation-generic symbol mapper in the test-side prototype (shipped path untouched), with two calibration lanes before it is trusted - (i) the generic prototype re-run on the WN8 corpses must reproduce W5b2's 112 canonical / 32 disjoint byte-identically (the mapper may not move QAM16), (ii) the truth-reconstruction residual lane on WN7 must validate the 8PSK templates and alignment. Specimens: the WN7 guard-pin corpses w0/b0 (oracle-residual block b5, ship 0 / oracle 15) and w1/b0 (ship 20), then the residual bursts the W0 census CSVs name (the 83 + 48 battery errors). Reads: prototype coded errors per block against ship. Kill/proceed (pre-committed): prototype pooled residual on the named bursts <= half of ship's -> **G1b**, a shipped port behind structural 8PSK scope, corpse -> battery -> byte-identity, the gate decision by the §5.3 rule on both families; prototype no better than ship on those blocks -> the B3.9 verdict is confirmed under the new class and banked as "WN7 needs added information, measured under both the DFE-chain class and the MFB class", and the program moves on. Budget: ~2 days instrument + measurement; G1b, if it fires, 2-3 days.

- **G2 - WN8 first-block bootstrap (M, L if it builds).** Question: what defeats block 0 on the three disjoint bursts, and which banked lever prices to the mask class on them? Method, in order: (a) anatomy of w1/b0, w2/b0, w3/b0 under the shipped decoder, reproduced byte-exactly on the prototype's rung logs (`MS110D_MFBRX_*`): delay-profile window placement (the B3.5b class, which bit the disjoint specimen once already), left-boundary trajectory extrapolation, the refit gate, or the schedule itself; (b) price the candidates against the W1 truth seam before building any - the preamble as a leftward anchor for block 0 (a label-free anchor that is already on the wire), W3's moment track as a mid-block anchor for block 0 only, fade-adaptive refit cadence against the slow-fade edge, a third schedule. Build only what prices <= ~2x mask-class on the three specimens; ship structurally scoped to QAM16, corpse -> battery. Exit rule: the W6 decision rule verbatim (both families §5.3-green at the 4.33M-bit budgets -> exit (i); otherwise exit (ii) with the new ceiling re-banked). Budget: ~2 days anatomy + pricing; 2-3 days build if it fires. Carried caveat: WN8 Poor stays sim-only unless H2 succeeds, so this leg is the completeness of the spec claim, not field capability.

- **G3 - production-path coverage (S).** Question: does every waveform survive `FrequencyShiftedModem` at the GB7RDG centre within 0.2 dB of its native AWGN knee, and does the shifted WN6 Poor point hold its mask? Method: extend the `MS110D_SHIFT_GATE=1` ladder to all ten waveforms including WN7/WN8; add a shifted WN6 Poor smoke to the hermetic set and a shifted full WN6 Poor point to the battery form; a journal line at config and SETHW time for WN7/WN8 stating their measured-only Poor status and the 2026-08-03 on-air ceiling, so the product says what the ledger says. Kill: any knee moved > 0.2 dB at a shifted centre is a receive-path defect in the shifter (the #221 first-cut lesson: noise folding before the downshift) and is fixed before the tenant goes on air.

- **H1 - a radio evening (Tom, 40 m, late afternoon or evening, never mid-morning).** In priority order, each with its own evidence dir: §E3 IQ-vs-DAX A/B (20 minutes; both routes are built and have never been run as a seeded pair); [ota-test-plan.md](ota-test-plan.md) Phase 1 proper - the hour-long mixed ladder with power varied, through the tenant config if possible, which is also the first on-air result at a moved centre; m9psy as second site if its quota allows. Standing rules from [ota-air-backlog.md](ota-air-backlog.md): dial correction per receiver, a tone before any ladder, the clear-frequency check covering 3 kHz either side, ident as M0LTE.

- **H2 - the pad-chain ceiling (Tom, studybox rig, ~1 hour, the highest-payoff hardware hour).** Question: is the RSP1 rig's ~13.5 dB-at-3.7 W deliverable SNR ceiling set by its gain stages or by thermal noise? Method: with nothing transmitting, capture the RSP1 noise floor at IFGR 20/40/59 across the RFGR states (`rx_sdr ... -g 'AGC=false,IFGR=<n>,RFGR=<m>'`) and tabulate dBFS vs gain; then a 15 W tone and burst at the best setting. If the floor is gain-stage limited, swap one 50 dB pad for 30 dB (a 105 dB chain puts ~-63 dBm at the RSP1 at 15 W, far below any overload) and re-measure. Target: >= 25 dB delivered, so that WN7 Poor (+19) and WN8 Poor (+23) can run through a real transmitter chain for the first time. Record: the floor-vs-gain table, the pad chain as built, FWDPWR, the new ceiling, and the `--rsp-gain` string that achieved it. This is the only route by which the two ledger rows ever move from "sim-only by physics" to rig-confirmed.

## 6. On-air honesty

WN7's and WN8's Poor mask regions (+19/+23 dB) sit above both rigs' ceilings as they stand, so any Poor gate this program produces is **sim-only** until H2 lifts the pad-chain ceiling; the [mode-validation.md](../mode-validation.md) row will say "sim hard-gated", cite the ceiling as the reason there is no hardware confirmation, and leave the on-air column unforwarded. A row never becomes an unqualified working on sim evidence alone. On a real NVIS path, 16QAM at 6400 bps did not carry at 48 W on 2026-08-03 while 8PSK did; that is the honest shape of the link budget, and the ledger rows say so.
