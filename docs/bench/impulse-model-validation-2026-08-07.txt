Impulse-noise model validation loop - 2026-08-07 (rx-roadmap workstream 6 item 2)

The injector (tests/Packet.SoundModem.Tests/Channel/ImpulseNoise.cs) is calibrated by a
closed loop: 30 minutes of its own output (850 Hz tone + AWGN at 10 dB SNR3k +
SunrisePeak-rate impulses; ImpulseNoiseTests.Synthesis_For_The_Validation_Loop with
IMPULSE_SYNTH_DIR set) re-analysed by the capture campaign's own measuring instrument
(docs/bench/impulse-stats-2026-08-06.py methodology). A model the instrument cannot
confirm is not calibrated.

Converged fit (broadband-event class, detected by the instrument):

  metric                measured 40 m target   injected, detected   delta
  rate (SunrisePeak)    220 /min               249 /min             +13 %
  amplitude p50         18.1 dB over bg        19.6                 +1.5 dB
  amplitude p90         23.8                   25.7                 +1.9 dB
  amplitude p99         34.7                   34.5                 -0.2 dB
  duration p50          19 ms                  20.5                 +8 %
  duration p90          102 ms                 130.9                +28 %
  duration p99          1835 ms                1465.8               -20 %

Instrument couplings learned through the loop, recorded so nobody re-fights them:
- The instrument's "dB over background" reads ~6 dB above the drawn broadband sigma
  ratio (p20-of-envelope background convention ~4 dB, peak-of-smoothed-envelope readout
  ~2 dB); the injector's amplitude base absorbs this (ThresholdDb 7.4 against the
  instrument's nominal 15.56).
- The two-band coincidence gate passes ~65-70 percent of drawn events at these
  amplitudes; the drawn rate is scaled so the DETECTED broadband rate matches the
  profile.
- Second-long measured events are crash TRAINS: only a dense volley of floored
  sub-crashes (spacing under the instrument's 20 ms merge gap) reproduces them; a single
  decaying envelope crosses back under detection within a fraction of its span. The
  volley's peak statistics couple train tuning to the amplitude percentiles, which is
  why the joint fit is frozen at the table above rather than chased further.
- The measured amplitude tail (max 73 dB) is an AGC-compressed lower bound per the
  original record; the model deliberately does not chase it.

Reproduce: build tests, then
  IMPULSE_SYNTH_DIR=<dir> <test-exe> -class Packet.SoundModem.Tests.Channel.ImpulseNoiseTests
  python3 docs/bench/impulse-stats-2026-08-06.py <dir>/raw-*.wav
(rate table note: the instrument's per-half-hour table divides by 30 minutes regardless
of coverage; with 10-minute synthetic chunks read the headline events line instead.)
