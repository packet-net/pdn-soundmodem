Your station

What this station is set up to do, in one place. Each area opens the settings behind it.

Radio and audio

A FlexRadio 6500 at 10.45.0.76, slice A, receiving and transmitting through DAX channel 1.

Dial 7.0500 MHz, upper sideband, 2.8 kHz wide.

Keying

The radio keys itself; this station asks it to transmit over the network.

Carrier sense listens to the audio.

Worth a look. No callsign ident is set. Stations that transmit unattended usually send one every 10 minutes.

Modes and frequencies

Three modems: NinoTNC 300 baud BPSK on 7.0508 MHz, NinoTNC 600 baud QPSK on 7.0516 MHz, and 300 baud HF packet on 7.0525 MHz.

All three fit inside the radio's passband.

Connect your software

Node and BBS software connect over KISS to port 8105 on every network interface.

LinBPQ is connected now, on sub-channels 0 and 1.

Sharing and logging

The station page is at port 8099 on your network, and asks for the key before any change.

Heard frames are kept for 30 days. Not sent to a public monitor.

2 changes not applied yet: ident every 10 minutes, KISS port 8105 on this machine only. Applying restarts the station for about 5 seconds. LinBPQ reconnects by itself.
