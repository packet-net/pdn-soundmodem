# G1d - the WN7 ensemble ships: DFE-chain + MFB per 8PSK block, residual selection (MS110D Poor-gate successor program)

Registered 2026-08-20 after G1c's PASS ([../2026-08-20-poorgate-g1/](../2026-08-20-poorgate-g1/)), per [poor-gate-successor-plan.md](../../poor-gate-successor-plan.md) §3/§4, before any shipped-path code.

## Registration

**Question.** Does the shipped receiver, running the MFB-form block decoder beside the DFE-chain path on every 8PSK block and keeping the decode with the lower label-free reconstruction residual, bring WN7 Poor to the D-LXIV mask on both seed families at full §5.3 budgets, with every non-8PSK point byte-identical?

**Decision inputs (banked).** G1: the two receivers are never wrong on the same block (0 of 88), and the MFB reconstruction residual selects a zero-error decode on all 88 (18 contested, margins +0.17 % to +22 %). G0: WN7 83/3,243,776 canonical and 48/3,243,776 disjoint; the eight bursts that carry them; the 120-census byte-identity baseline; the five §6 guard pins (WN7 w0/b0 0 coded, WN7 w1/b0 20 coded - both expected to move under this change, the other three exact by structural scope). W5b2: the shipped `Ms110dMfbBlockDecoder` (caps SoftCap 30 / TotalCap 72, refit gate 5 %, cycle-accept) and its measured wall-clock class.

**Mechanism claim.** The chain path's WN7 residual is deep-fade decoder events (1-32 info bits, honest low-|LLR| wire bits); the MFB's is basin traps (confident wrong fixed points) on different blocks. Each re-encodes to wire symbols the other receiver's trajectory does not explain, so the block's reconstruction residual through the MFB's probe-anchored trajectory prices a wrong decode above a right one by the energy of its wire-symbol difference, with a cross-term noise far below that energy at every contested block measured.

**Instrument + method.**
1. `Ms110dMfbBlockDecoder` takes the modulation-generic symbol map the prototype proved (calibration lane (i): its QAM16 arithmetic must stay operation-identical; the WN8 corpses 509/10509 and the WN8 battery points must reproduce 112/32 and 1,254/75,713 byte-identically) and gains `Price(info)`: the mean reconstruction residual of a given block decode through the block's final anchor set.
2. `Ms110dDemodulator`: structurally scoped to `Psk8` blocks that satisfy the existing turbo gate, after the chain path's decode is final (converged, salvaged or reverted): run the MFB on a copy, price both decodes, keep the lower residual. Ties keep the chain's. A new report-only counter (`MfbSelected`) surfaces in the autopsy summary as its own line; the census line format and the `turbo` field are unchanged so byte-identity stays meaningful.
3. Order: hermetic suite; the eight G1 specimens as corpses (expected 0 on every block, matching the instrument); the five §6 guard pins (three exact, the two WN7 pins re-recorded); per-block wall-clock of the MFB at WN7 on this box (report-only, against the 7.68 s a WN7 block takes on air); then the full three-lane battery in the G0 form.

**Kill/proceed rule (pre-committed).**
- Battery: WN7 Poor both families clear the §5.3 rule (<= 32 errors direct on 3,243,776 bits, or the 97.5 % Poisson bound <= 1E-5 at fewer), AWGN WN7 0, all 112 non-WN7 censuses byte-identical to G0, WN8 censuses byte-identical to G0 (the generic map may not move QAM16), WN6/WN13/WN0 pins exact -> **exit (i) for WN7**: `Poor_Channel_Mask_Gate` arms WN7 by default, `MeasuredOnlyBank` drops its WN7 rows, the ledger row forwards to sim hard-gated (on-air column unforwarded per plan §6), guard pins re-recorded, the B4 false-red check at the registered budget.
- WN7 improved but not §5.3-green on both families -> **exit (ii)**: the ensemble ships if every corpse and the battery show it never selects a wrong decode that the chain path had right (a selector that loses blocks is worse than no selector); the new counts re-bank in `MeasuredOnlyBank`; the closeout states the new ceiling.
- Any wrong selection on a block the chain path had right, or any non-WN7 census moved -> stop, diagnose in writing, no merge.

## Measurements (2026-08-20)

**The build (commit 811233a).** `Ms110dMfbBlockDecoder` takes the prototype's modulation-generic symbol map and gains `Price(info, frameChips)` (re-encode, reconstruct through the block's final anchor set, mean residual over the data span) and `LastDecode`. `Ms110dDemodulator`: after the chain path's decode is final on an 8PSK block (converged, salvaged or reverted), the MFB runs on a copy; if and only if it converged (exact fixed point or accepted cycle), both decodes are priced and the lower residual is kept, a tie keeping the chain's. `MfbOffered` / `MfbSelected` are report-only counters, surfaced as their own line in the autopsy summary; the census format and the `turbo` field are unchanged.

**What the hermetic suite caught, and the two things it taught.** The first build failed exactly one hermetic case: `Interleaver_And_Constraint_Length_Variants_Loop_Back(wn: 7, UltraShort, k: 7)` returned no burst at all. Diagnostics (`FrameDiagnostics` on that loopback): block 0 of the burst never settled in the soft phase (churn 150-165 of 576 bits for thirty rungs), was reported `conv=2` at rung 30, and priced at 1.60E-2 against the chain's correct decode at 3.99E-2, so the ensemble took the garbage decode and the burst's EOM was never found.

1. *The model was wrong, not the selector.* The MFB took every probe preceding a block's first frame to be a boundary (cyclically shifted) probe whenever the interleaver has one frame per block - `(p + 1) % frames == 0` is true for all p at frames = 1 - but the probe that ends the preamble is emitted unshifted, so block 0's first anchor was fitted against the wrong 32-chip sequence, and with only two anchors per block the whole model was garbage. Under a garbage model a correct decode prices no better than a wrong one, which is why the residual comparison meant nothing there. `ProbeIsBoundary(p, frames)` now follows the modulator exactly (block 0's preceding probe is never a boundary probe); for every interleaver with more than one frame per block its output is identical to the W5b2 expression, so the Long-interleaver batteries are unmoved by construction. With the fix the block converges in two rungs and both decodes price at 1.116E-6. The prototype carries the same correction. The W5b2 QAM16 path had the same latent fault for UltraShort, where the revert principle would have hidden it.
2. *The convergence gate was right to add and not sufficient on its own.* The first gate attempt (only a converged MFB decode is offered) did not cure the loopback, because the period-2 cycle-accept fired at the soft-to-hard handover rung against soft-phase decodes and called a 156-bit oscillation converged. That is a latent hole in the shipped QAM16 path too and can move WN8 numbers, so it is filed as #316 for its own registration rather than fixed here. The gate stays: the B3.3 principle, and the condition under which G1 measured the residual discriminating.

**Calibration on the way (ungated and gated builds, before the probe fix; the fix cannot touch Long-interleaver blocks):** the eight WN7 Poor specimens all decode to **0 coded errors** through the shipped ensemble (the chain alone left 131), with `selected` per burst exactly the count of blocks the chain had wrong in G1 (2, 2, 1, 1, 0, 2, 3, 2); the WN8 corpses reproduce **112 canonical / 32 disjoint** to the digit (the generic map left QAM16 alone); the WN6, WN13sp and WN0 guard pins exact; the WN7 w0/b0 pin stays 0 coded with `oracle b5:15` and its `turbo 11c/0r/4v` unchanged (offered 11, selected 0); the WN7 w1/b0 pin moves from 20 coded to **0** (offered 11, selected 2), its `turbo 11c/0r/5v` unchanged.

**Wall-clock class (report-only, under 10-way parallel load on this box):** a WN7 Poor corpse burst (11 blocks, ~90 s of air) ran 70-112 s with the ensemble against 50-75 s for the chain alone on the same box at G0, i.e. the MFB adds roughly 0.3-0.5x of air time per block on a loaded core.

### Battery attempt 1 (residual-only selector, build 811233a; [battery-attempt1/](battery-attempt1/))

**Poor WN7: 0 / 3,243,776 canonical and 0 / 3,243,776 disjoint** (from 83 and 48), both lanes rc=1 because `MeasuredOnlyBank` asserted the banked counts, as it should. All 113 non-WN7 censuses byte-identical to G0 (WN8 included: the generic map left QAM16 alone). **AWGN WN7: 8 errors where G0 had 0** - one burst (w0/b0, channelSeed 108), block 8. That is the registered stop condition ("any wrong selection on a block the chain path had right"), so: no merge, diagnose in writing.

**Diagnosis.** A new `MS110D_AUTOPSY_AWGN=1` rig reproduces the mask suite's AWGN bursts as corpses (seed base 100 + wn, empty path set); the failing burst reproduced to the bit (8 coded errors, first 320244, last 320258). On block 8 the MFB converged to a decode that differs from the chain's in **5 wire bits** - a nearest-neighbour codeword, the signature of a maximum-likelihood error rather than a basin trap - and priced it 0.014 % below the chain's correct decode (2.495510E-3 against 2.495849E-3). That is not a selector accident; it is structural. The MFB's converged decode is, by construction, approximately the codeword that minimises the reconstruction residual under its own model. When such a decode is wrong because the noise realisation favoured it, the residual favours it too, so a residual comparison can never see an MFB maximum-likelihood error. The residual saw the MFB's *Poor* failures only because those were basin traps whose co-adapted model still fitted the truth better (5-22 % excess). The chain's failures are a different estimator's errors and the MFB residual catches them, which is the asymmetry G1 measured without understanding it.

What does see the AWGN error is the chain's own evidence. The chain's final wire LLRs at the five disagreeing bits average **4.46** (about 99 % sure of each); at the bits where the chain is *wrong* on the Poor specimens they average 1.1-3.4 (B3.9's "honest erasures near 1"). Putting both receivers' evidence in the same units - the MFB's as its Gaussian log-likelihood gain, rows x ln(chain residual / MFB residual), the chain's as the sum of |LLR| over the disagreeing wire bits - separates every contested block ([selection-evidence.txt](selection-evidence.txt), 19 blocks):

| class | blocks | chain evidence (sum abs LLR at diff) | MFB evidence (rows x ln ratio) | winner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chain wrong, MFB right (Poor) | 13 | 4.5 to 26.1 | 64.9 to 880.1 | MFB, by 8x at the thinnest (507 w1/b1 b4: 7.9 vs 64.9) |
| MFB wrong, chain right (AWGN, ML error) | 1 | 22.3 | 5.0 | chain, by 4.5x |
| MFB wrong, chain right (Poor, basin traps) | 5 | 246 to 1007 | -1713 to -5920 | chain, trivially |

**The rule (commit that follows 811233a): switch to the MFB's decode iff the MFB converged, the chain's LLR buffer is complete, the two decodes differ, and MFB evidence > chain evidence.** No constant is tuned; both sides are log-likelihoods under each receiver's own model. Hermetic MS110D namespace 297/0 and `SourceTextTests` green on the rule build; the second cycle (the nine corpses including the AWGN burst, the five pins, the full battery) follows.
